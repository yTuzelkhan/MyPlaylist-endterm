About the website

MyPlaylist brings our music choices together in one place. It includes Tame Impala, Ayau, Kairat Nurtas, The Weeknd, Dua Lipa, and Billie Eilish.

You can explore songs, discover albums, and choose your favorite music.

What you can explore

  • A collection of artists on the home page
  • A table of songs with listening links
  • Six albums from different artists
  • A page dedicated to the artists
